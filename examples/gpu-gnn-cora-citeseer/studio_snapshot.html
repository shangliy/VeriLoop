Why VeriLoop Studio vs. Black-Box Runners

Unlike black-box idea-to-paper runners that only surface a final PDF after hours of unverified LLM execution, VeriLoop Studio enforces mechanical accountability at every stage:

  • Formula ↔ Code AST Alignment: Parses arXiv LaTeX sources directly and maps equations to baseline AST symbols.
  • Byte-Deterministic Binding: TensorBoard / W&B / CSV logs bind directly into bound_macros.tex and Okabe-Ito vector PDFs.
  • Dual-Layer Hard Blocking: Layer 1 catches AST cheating (0.0% FPR), sign flips, and fake references at $0 before paid Layer-2 review.
  • Interactive Click-to-Verify: Every number and citation in the Audit Workbench jumps straight to its raw log:line.
