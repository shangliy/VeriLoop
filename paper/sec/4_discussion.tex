\paragraph{Why frozen-artifact evaluation and unified rules matter.}
Independent hostile milestone audits during development uncovered four hazards common in governance benchmarking:
(1) defining ``leakage'' as \emph{flagged and unblocked} guarantees 0\% leakage for any blocking switch regardless of yield;
(2) re-running executors per condition confounds artifact drift with reviewer capability;
(3) labeling an unlogged analytic derivation as defective on one split (\texttt{H06}) while treating an equivalent derivation as a benign FP on another (\texttt{04c}) creates circular Layer-1 wins; and
(4) computing paired tests across $k=3$ repeats of one workspace causes pseudo-replication.
Freezing SHA-256 workspaces, applying one unified post-audit rule (with dual-label sensitivity in Table~\ref{tab:app_sensitivity}), pinning locked v2 gate snapshots, and reporting workspace-level CIs and McNemar tests directly address these hazards.

\paragraph{Practical deployment rule.}
Because locked Layer~1 runs in milliseconds at \$0 cost, blocks 5/7 (71.4\%, 4/7 attributable) frozen defects in sample, and achieves 0.0\% unseen AST FPR, production harnesses should hard-block on AST rules and route signed number tracing (\texttt{trace\_numbers.py}) as \$0 diagnostics into Layer~2 (reserving strict numerical hard blocks for log-every-number contracts).

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Five boundaries scope our findings:
(1) \emph{Small sample \& single-team annotation:} Our 16 frozen workspaces (9 \textsc{Clean}, 7 \textsc{Defective}; 2 natural defects \texttt{H05}/\texttt{C05}) lack blind non-author inter-rater reliability; \texttt{H06} was relabeled post hoc (Table~\ref{tab:app_sensitivity}), and workspace tests ($p=0.500000$--$1.000000$) are underpowered.
(2) \emph{In-sample recall \& unmeasured unseen recall:} Locked v2 rules (\texttt{b897378}) were finalized with the 16 workspaces visible, while 18/18 unseen runs avoided traps, calibrating out-of-sample FPR rather than recall.
(3) \emph{Post-v2 tracer updates:} Shipped v3 \texttt{trace\_numbers.py} (\texttt{--data}, unsigned prose polarity) awaits a fresh held-out benchmark before claiming v3 FP reductions.
(4) \emph{Incomplete cells:} \texttt{codex-cli} \texttt{with\_summary} stopped at 5/48 trials (quota cap; 43 errors retained), and non-interactive \texttt{gemini} CLI awaits cloud setup.
(5) \emph{Workload scope:} Controlled adversarial benchmarks remain CPU-only; while Appendix~\ref{app:gpu_case_study} demonstrates live VeriLoop governance on a 42-run RTX 3060~Ti study, multi-node distributed GPU workloads remain future work.
