\paragraph{Why frozen-artifact evaluation matters for research governance.}
During development of this benchmark, hostile log-level auditing uncovered three evaluation pitfalls common in agentic benchmarking:
(1) defining ``leakage'' as \emph{flagged and unblocked} mechanically guarantees 0\% leakage for any blocking switch regardless of clean-output yield;
(2) re-running executors separately per reviewer condition confounds run-to-run artifact drift with reviewer capability or framing effects; and
(3) tuning deterministic checkers on benchmark runs without reporting workspace-level CIs or out-of-sample flag rates overstates mechanical generalization.
Freezing SHA-256 workspaces, committing gate-independent \texttt{file:line} ground truth, forbidding aggregation overrides, and locking gates before unseen blind testing directly address these hazards and motivate our \texttt{milestone-dual-audit} checkpoint skill.

\paragraph{Practical deployment rule.}
Because Layer~1 executes in milliseconds at zero API cost with 0.0\% AST flag rate and 0.69\% number-level benign flag rate out of sample, production harnesses should run Layer~1 first to fail fast on unlogged decimals, sign flips, and mock fallbacks (indexing both \texttt{out/} and \texttt{data/} to avoid raw-input quote warnings), and then invoke a read-only Layer-2 auditor to catch code-logic and inferential defects.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Five boundaries scope our findings:
(1) \emph{Small workspace sample and provenance imbalance:} Our frozen corpus comprises 6 natural runs, 5 clean twin bases, and 5 constructed defect twins (only 3 natural defects total, 2 in the Haiku stratum and 1 in the Codex stratum). Consequently, the recall lift of \texttt{L1 $\cup$ codex-cli} over \texttt{codex-cli} alone rests on one workspace (\texttt{H06}, paired $p=0.250000$), and larger multi-annotator natural corpora are required for statistical power.
(2) \emph{In-sample Layer-1 Table~\ref{tab:primary} metrics and unlabeled unseen traps:} Layer-1 rules were finalized in commit \texttt{b897378} with the frozen corpus visible, making Table~\ref{tab:primary} Layer-1 figures in-sample; meanwhile, our 21-scenario unseen blind test verified the 2 triggered flags forensically as benign but did not independently label all 18 reports for semantic traps, yielding flag rates rather than full out-of-sample recall.
(3) \emph{Incomplete third-family and Codex summary cells:} \texttt{codex-cli} \texttt{with\_summary} completed 5/48 trials before hitting the provider 5-hour quota cap (43 error rows retained verbatim), and local \texttt{gemini} CLI non-interactive auditing awaits cloud project configuration.
(4) \emph{Residual Layer-1 benign flags:} Three edge cases remain in frozen \texttt{trace\_numbers.py}: prose-polarity phrasing (\texttt{C04}: ``1.4598 percentage points below'' vs.\ \texttt{-1.4598}), unlogged analytic parameter expressions (\texttt{04c}: \texttt{0.425}), and raw CSV quotes when \texttt{data/} is excluded (\texttt{09c}: \texttt{3.542}).
(5) \emph{Workload scope:} All benchmarks are CPU-only tabular, numerical, and symbolic tasks; multi-GPU deep-learning workloads remain future work.
