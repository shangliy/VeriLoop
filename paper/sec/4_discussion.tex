\paragraph{Architecture vs.\ model scale.}
Current LLMs (\texttt{claude-haiku}, \texttt{codex-cli}) avoided 0/16 coarse \textsc{SPR} pitfalls and most obvious \textsc{SciIntegrity} refusal traps, unlike earlier systems in \citet{luo2025pitfalls}, yet still committed verifiable fine-grained fabrications in over 80\% of unguarded Round-1 runs.
Scaling shifts failures from blatant refusals to subtler denominator bugs, unvalidated intervals, and log contradictions.
Pairing a fast executor (\texttt{claude-haiku}, \$0.02--\$0.06/run) with mechanical gates and a strict cross-family auditor (\texttt{codex-cli}) delivers higher verified integrity per dollar than an unguarded frontier model.

\paragraph{Decoupling misconduct from sufficiency.}
Our \textsc{SPR} study highlights a key governance rule: anti-fabrication blocking (\texttt{BLOCK\_MISCONDUCT}) should be decoupled from publication-sufficiency gating (\texttt{BLOCK\_UNSUPPORTED} for multi-seed or external baseline gaps).
Enforcing full publication sufficiency under a 60s CPU cap withheld 3/4 repaired \textsc{SPR} reports even after all misconduct was fixed and verified \textsc{Pass} by the blind judge; production harnesses should unconditionally block misconduct while making sufficiency checks budget-configurable.

\paragraph{Limitations.}
Four limitations bound our findings:
(1) \emph{Tracer edge cases:} Live runs exposed four formatting edge cases in \texttt{trace\_numbers.py} (trailing-zero decimals \texttt{1.000} vs.\ \texttt{1.0} on \texttt{05a}, Unicode minus \texttt{U+2212}, spaced subtraction \texttt{53.989 - 0.4438*P}, and ASCII \texttt{+/-} spacing on \texttt{10a}), all fixed with unit tests (cutting pilot false positives from 10/24 to 0/24); prose polarity (``below SOTA by 1.46 pp'' vs.\ \texttt{-1.4598}) remains as a low-severity warning case.
(2) \emph{Reviewer asymmetry and cost:} Strictness varies sharply (\texttt{codex-cli} $\gg$ \texttt{claude-sonnet} $\gg$ \texttt{claude-haiku}), and strict multi-turn review drives most API spend.
(3) \emph{Single-task ablation timeout:} Expanded ablations covered 11/12 defective primary tasks; the 12th slot (\texttt{SPR\_PostHocSelection} under \texttt{Ours\_ccr}) hit the 600s CPU timeout on an unvectorized Python loop.
(4) \emph{Scope:} Two model families, one revision turn, and CPU-only benchmarks leave multi-round convergence, third-family auditors, and multi-GPU deep-learning workloads for future work.
