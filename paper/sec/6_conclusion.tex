\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Autonomous research agents rarely fail by omitting files; they fail by delivering complete-looking reports containing subtle numerical, coding, and inferential fabrications.
Across 112 controlled runs spanning all 11 \textsc{SciIntegrity-Bench} traps and all 4 \textsc{AIScientistPitfalls} pitfalls (\$46.4928 total), existence gates blocked 0/15 defective outputs, advisory review leaked 13/15 (86.7\%) blockers, same-model review dropped blocking to 18.2\% ($p=0.003906$) with 5/11 sycophantic approvals, and executor summaries cut misconduct verdicts from 90.9\% to 36.4\% ($p=0.031250$).
Coupling deterministic evidence gates with path-isolated cross-family hard blocking and closed-loop repair cuts blocker leakage to 0/15 ($p=0.000244$), repairs \textsc{SPR} misconduct to 4/4 condition-blind \textsc{Pass} in one turn, and protects scientific integrity even under lenient LLM auditors.
