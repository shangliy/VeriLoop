\section{Long-Horizon Real-GPU Deep Learning Case Study}
\label{app:gpu_case_study}

To validate VeriLoop's end-to-end pipeline---log-to-\LaTeX{} binding (\texttt{paper\_bind.py}), online citation verification (\texttt{citation\_audit.py}), expanded-PDF auditing (\texttt{pdf\_audit.py}), and interactive split-pane review (\texttt{review\_ui.py})---on a sustained deep learning workload, we ran 42 multi-seed PyTorch AMP fp16 jobs on a local NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3060~Ti (5548.4~s / 92.47~min compute, 593.0~MB peak VRAM; full artifact and committed gate/PDF/citation JSON logs under \texttt{examples/gpu-overnight-research/audits/}).
Four hypotheses were registered in \texttt{claims.json} prior to training (committed alongside results; future studies should anchor \texttt{claims.json} in a pre-run commit); every checkpoint and post-hoc temperature $T^*$ was selected strictly on the validation split; and held-out test metrics were scored once and logged to TensorBoard, W\&B, CSV, and an append-only ledger.
Across all 14 stages (8 bundled CIFAR-10 recipe stages + 6 Fashion-MNIST stages), VeriLoop updated \texttt{wiki/RESEARCH\_WIKI.md} and ran a live pipeline self-consistency and AST smoke check: 14/14 stages passed (182 distinct numbers in the final wiki re-audited across stages, 0 AST \texttt{HIGH}, 81.8~ms median latency at \$0 API cost; because the wiki and \texttt{wiki\_metrics.log} are emitted together, this validates pipeline integrity rather than tracing recall, while \texttt{audits/pdf\_audit.json} independently traces 100\% of expanded PDF decimals with 0 untraced and 0 sign mismatches).
Table~\ref{tab:app_gpu} summarizes the multi-seed results, including the Label Smoothing underconfidence reversal ($T^*=0.7234$ on CIFAR-10; $0.6716$ on Fashion-MNIST) and CIFAR-10 Mixup reversal ($T^*=0.6449$), contrasted with overconfident unaugmented \texttt{M2\_ResNet\_Base} ($T^*=2.6460$ on CIFAR-10; $1.3368$ on Fashion-MNIST).
