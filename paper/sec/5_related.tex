\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Autonomous research harnesses.}
Table~\ref{tab:related} contrasts VeriLoop against prior harnesses and auditing suites.
\textsc{ARIS}~\citep{yang2026aris} introduces a 65-skill workflow yet keeps Stage-1 experiment auditing advisory on a single unbaselined run.
\textsc{EviBound}~\citep{chen2026evibound} enforces pre-execution schemas and MLflow existence gates (0/8 missing-file hallucinations on eight Colab tasks), without checking calculation logic, numerical provenance, or inferential consistency inside executed code.

\paragraph{Fabrication and error-auditing benchmarks.}
\textsc{SciIntegrity-Bench}~\citep{yang2026sciintegrity} evaluates seven LLMs on 33 dilemmatic scenarios (11 traps, 34.2\% failure rate; removing pressure barely changes synthetic substitution from 57.1\% to 55.6\%).
\citet{luo2025pitfalls} show on \textsc{SPR} that auditing code plus execution logs lifts detection from 55.0\% to 82.0\% ($p=6.3\times 10^{-5}$).
\textsc{FabScore}~\citep{chen2026fabscore} audits 144 AI papers post-hoc (21.2\% claim fabrication; 85.9\% contradicting logs; 44.6\% hardcoded/mocked code; 86.2\% missed by AI reviewers), and manuscript-only suites \textsc{FLAWS}~\citep{xi2025flaws} (713 ICML papers) and \textsc{PaperAudit-Bench}~\citep{tu2026paperaudit} (220 papers, $>$2,200 errors) confirm text-only review cannot verify execution.

\paragraph{Cross-model verification.}
\citet{song2026secondmodel} evaluates 900 sessions on 30 artifacts, finding single-pass cross-model review does not uniformly beat same-model review whereas combining both lifts recall from 42.7\% to 56.7\% ($p=0.006$).
Our frozen evaluation extends this paradigm to autonomous scientific workspaces, quantifying zero-cost mechanical pre-filtering, strict-blocking yield trade-offs, and reviewer-calibration differences on natural code bugs.
