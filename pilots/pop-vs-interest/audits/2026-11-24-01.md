## 3. Findings (Ordered by Severity)

### Blockers

1. **[C2] Single-seed only (`s0`) on a single dataset (`G2` open)**
   - **Evidence**: `tiger_subtree_gr.py:458`, `geotrie_ablations.py:350,357`, `claims.json:106,115,124`, `reports/2026-11-24-01.md:53-72`.
   - **Smallest fix**: Train and evaluate `GeoTrie-GR (la00)` and its key ablations across 3 seeds (`s0, s1, s2`) on Beauty (and at least one other dataset, e.g., Office or Industrial), or explicitly downgrade `C2` to `pilot` in the ledger and report.

2. **[C2] Unmatched `tau` / non-telescoping `beta_inf` in Table 2.1 ("Matched-`tau` Frontier")**
   - **Evidence**: `tiger_subtree_gr.py:243-247` adds `beta_inf * gdata.log_mean_cnt[l][cn_safe]` at **all 4 depths inside `log_softmax`** without subtracting the parent's `log_mean_cnt` (unlike true telescoping post-softmax in `beauty_tiger_3seed_eval.py:47-57`). Meanwhile, `audit_and_frontier_logs.py:122` applies `-tau * LP` once at the leaf to exact `S_tiger_beauty_full.npy`. Thus `beta_inf = -tau` applies ~4× different popularity reweighting plus 4 renormalizations; Table 2.1 (`reports/2026-11-24-01.md:53-61`) is NOT a matched-`tau` comparison. (Note: the full All-Tail curve of `GeoTrie-GR` still lies above `TIGER`—e.g., at matched Tail `~0.153`, GeoTrie `beta=-0.25` gets `0.3122/0.1552` vs TIGER `tau=1.00` `0.2367/0.1526`—so the curve dominance holds, not the matched-`tau` row-wise CIs.)
   - **Smallest fix**: In `GeoTrieGR.beam_decode`, subtract `tau * (ch_lmc - par_lmc)` *after* `log_softmax` (matching `beauty_tiger_3seed_eval.py:47-57`) and recompute Table 2.1 CIs (also using beam-400 on both sides rather than exact TIGER vs beam-200 GeoTrie).

3. **[C2] Simple baselines already in own logs beat `GeoTrie-GR` at matched Tail**
   - **Evidence**:
     - Training-free `hybrid-kNN (w128, d0.6)` (`qtext_and_matched_ci.txt:44`) gets **All `0.2937`, Head `0.3718`, Tail `0.2119`**, beating `GeoTrie-GR (beta=-1.00)` (`audit_and_frontier_logs.txt:90`: **All `0.2752`, Head `0.3320`, Tail `0.2119`**) by **`+0.0185` All / `+0.0398` Head at identical Tail**.
     - Flat `fullD noID+L2 + IVF` over the exact same d=1 TIGER buckets (`ivf_and_fulld_control.txt:24`, 26.8% scan, `0.051 ms/q`, **30× faster**) gets **All `0.3085`, Tail `0.1950`**, beating `GeoTrie-GR (beta=-0.75)` (`audit_and_frontier_logs.txt:83`: **All `0.2914`, Tail `0.1943`**) by **`+0.0171` All at matched Tail**.
     - Neither comparison appears in Section 2 of `reports/2026-11-24-01.md:51-72`.
   - **Smallest fix**: Add both `hybrid-kNN` and `fullD noID+L2 + IVF` to Table 2.1 (with matched-Tail comparisons and measured latency) and explicitly bound `C2` as *"improves autoregressive Trie-GR over TIGER, but does not beat training-free `hybrid-kNN` or flat `noID+L2 + IVF` at high-Tail operating points."*

4. **[C3] Post-hoc claim revision without confirmatory evidence (`G6` open)**
   - **Evidence**: `claims.json:172-181`, `claims.py check`: `FAIL G6 claim fixed before evidence: revised 2026-11-24 20:41:30, last evidence 2026-11-24 20:41:30`. `C3` originally claimed Pareto dominance over both `ID+unnorm` and `noID+L2` across sequential datasets; after `fg_noid_all3.txt` (0/3 strict dominance on Office/Industrial, 2/3 on Beauty) and `microlens_benchmark.txt` (Tail collapse to `0.0269`), it was narrowed to `(Beauty, Industrial)` vs `ID+unnorm` only.

6. **[C1, C2] Weak/mismatched `TIGER` baseline (selection metric, input modality, published-number gap)**
   - **Evidence**: `exp_gr/src/train.py:57-61` selects TIGER on `val_R@10` (`s1` peaked ep 55, dropping test R@200 to `0.2511`), vs `val_R@200` for `fullD` & `GeoTrie-GR`. Input mismatch: continuous 768-D text vs discrete SIDs; Office/Industrial TIGER are 1-seed only (`liger_baseline.txt:3,13`).
   - **Smallest fix**: Add `val_R@200`-selected TIGER control on Beauty, 3 seeds on Office/Industrial, and disclose input-modality and RQ-KMeans vs RQ-VAE gaps.

7. **[C1] 2×2 `ID/noID` × `unnorm/L2` ablation is single-seed, uses `L=20` vs `L=10`, and hides Office & Beauty counterexamples**
   - **Evidence**: `noid_norm_ablation.py:149,175` ran 1 seed (`s0`) with `L=20` vs `L=10` in `fg_noid_all3.py:158`. On Office (`noid_norm_ablation.txt:5-6`, `ivf_and_fulld_control.txt:4`), `noID+unnorm` beats `noID+L2` on BOTH All and Tail (`0.432/0.217` vs `0.4294/0.2002`); on Beauty (`noid_norm_ablation.txt:21-22`), `noID+unnorm` beats `noID+L2` on Tail (`0.2005` vs `0.1980`).
   - **Smallest fix**: Run 3-seed 2×2 factorial at consistent `L`, include Office & Industrial in Table 1.3, and attribute Tail gains to removing IDs.

8. **[C2] Multi-change ablations in Table 2.2 leave key confounds unisolated**
   - **Evidence**: `tiger_subtree_gr.py:146-215` vs `gr.py:111-141` changes >=6 things simultaneously; no ablation isolates local Trie-child masked CE training vs global-vocab CE, and `geotrie_cum_rq` / `geotrie_resid_cb` (`geotrie_ablations.py:259-270`) conflate L0–L2 centroids with L3 lossless leaf vectors. `geotrie_no_logS` vs `geotrie_la00` `ΔAll = -0.0017 [-0.0059,+0.0025]` is non-significant on All (`geotrie_ablations.txt:34`).
   - **Smallest fix**: Add 1-variable ablations for local Trie-child CE and L0–L2-only quantized centroids keeping L3 lossless leaf text.

9. **[C3] Missing frequency-gate ablation, test-side `tau` cherry-picking, metric omission (`R@20`), grid-edge `alpha_id`**
